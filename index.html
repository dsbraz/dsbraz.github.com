I design software systems and the organizations that build them

Patterns I keep seeing

I treat architecture and organization as one design problem. That means clear ownership, boundaries that let teams ship without waiting on each other, and reliability that is built in instead of depending on heroics. When a decision is hard and technical, I want to be in it, and I want the team to be able to make the next one without me.

I’m CTO of BRQ’s Digital Products Studio, where I lead the teams that build and run dozens of products used by millions of people. Lately, most of my attention goes to AI agents in production: where they fit, how their work is measured, how their autonomy grows. I still write code, mostly Go and Python, mostly AI agents.

Before BRQ, I built platforms inside product companies. At Senior Sistemas, as General Manager of X Platform, I took it from zero to a business unit. At Nuvemshop, I led forward-deployed engineering globally: the teams, APIs, SDKs, test environments and partners that let large brands implement and extend the platform. Earlier, eight years at Accenture, as Technology Director for financial services and telecom.
